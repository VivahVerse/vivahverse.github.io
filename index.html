How it works

Three steps to a finished invitation

  1. 1

    Choose a design

    Pick your tradition and one of 95 designs. We fill in a blessing, wording and the usual functions, and you can change or remove any of them.

  2. 2

Help

Questions, troubleshooting & fine print

Is it really free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it's free, and there is no account. Everything runs in your browser. Drafts are saved locally on this device.

Where are my designs saved?

In this browser's local storage (IndexedDB), on this device only. They are not synced to your other devices. Clearing site data or using private browsing removes them. Use My Designs → Export backup to keep a copy, and Import to restore it on another device.

Can I write the invitation in Hindi? Is there auto-translation?

You can write in English, Hindi, or both. There is no automatic translation. You type or paste the Hindi wording yourself (manual mode, works offline). We supply editable Hindi phrases for common lines. More scripts (Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam) can be added later because the app is built for it.

Which music can I use? Can I use a song from YouTube?

Choose one of nine original tracks composed for this site (free to use), or upload a song file you have the right to use. You can also paste a YouTube or Spotify link: the song then plays on your wedding website through the official player. We can't copy audio from YouTube or Spotify into the MP4, because their terms forbid downloading and most film songs are copyrighted. To use a popular song in your video, download the MP4 without music and add the song from Instagram's or WhatsApp's own music library when you post.

Is this only for Hindu weddings?

No. There are presets for Hindu weddings (North Indian, Tamil, Telugu, Malayali, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Odia, Assamese, Bihari, Rajasthani, Kashmiri) and for Sikh, Muslim, Christian, Jain, Buddhist, Parsi and non-religious weddings. Each sets a suitable blessing, functions and design, and you can change everything.

Can I add a Ram Charit Manas chaupai or a Vedic mantra?

Yes. In Your details → Blessing, mantra or verse, choose from wedding chaupais and dohas from the Bal Kand (for example “मंगल भवन अमंगल हारी” or “सिय रघुबीर बिबाहु…”) or Vedic vivah mantras (मङ्गलं भगवान् विष्णुः, Rigveda 10.85 “इहैव स्तं…”, Saptapadi, Gayatri), each with transliteration and meaning. In Style & music, the video can open with the verse shown large. You can also type your own verse.

Why did my video download as WebM instead of MP4?

Chrome, Edge and Safari build a true MP4 with the browser's video encoder, usually faster than the video plays. Firefox can only make WebM. WhatsApp and Instagram accept MP4 most reliably, so if you get WebM, open the site in Chrome, Edge or Safari.

My download looks blurry or the text is cut off

Exports render at full resolution (for example 1500 × 2100 px for 5×7 in at 300 dpi), not as a screenshot of the preview. Text is shrunk automatically so it never runs past the frame. If the preview shows a “text was shortened” notice, reduce the wording or hide some optional lines in Customize → Visibility. If downloads don't start on iPhone, use Share instead, or long-press the image to save it.

Why can't I attach the image directly in a WhatsApp link?

WhatsApp links can only carry text. To send the image or video, use the Share button, which opens your phone's share sheet where it's supported, or download the file and attach it in WhatsApp.

Can guests RSVP online?

The static version gives you a WhatsApp RSVP link with a pre-filled message, plus your phone and email. Online RSVP forms that collect and sync responses need a backend or a form service, so they're not included and we don't pretend otherwise.

Browser compatibility

Works in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari (desktop and mobile). PNG, JPEG and PDF export work everywhere. Video export needs MediaRecorder: MP4 in recent Chrome, Edge and Safari, WebM in Firefox. Sharing files through the share sheet is supported on most phones and some desktop browsers. Otherwise, download and attach the file.

Privacy

This site has no server-side code, analytics or trackers. Your names, photos, audio and wording are processed in your browser and are never uploaded. The only network requests are for this site's own files. Fonts are bundled locally. See the full privacy notice and terms of use.
