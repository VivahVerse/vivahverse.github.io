Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Shubh Vivah Studio runs entirely in your web browser. Names, dates, addresses, phone numbers, photographs and audio that you enter or upload are processed on your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored, and where

  • Drafts are saved in your browser's IndexedDB storage on this device. They are not synced to other devices or to any server.
  • Preferences (light/dark theme, last opened design) are saved in your browser's localStorage.
  • Uploaded photos are resized in your browser and stored with your draft. Uploaded audio is stored separately in the same local storage.
  • You can remove everything at any time with My Designs → Reset all local data, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Network requests

The site loads only its own files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts and images) from the server it is hosted on. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and fonts are bundled locally rather than loaded from a font service. The hosting provider (for example GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses, as described in its own privacy policy.

When you share

Sharing is something you start yourself. When you open a WhatsApp link, use your device's share sheet or upload a downloaded file somewhere, the content goes to that service under its own terms. A wedding website package you host yourself is public to anyone who has the link.

Backups

Design backup files (.json) contain your text and photos. Keep them private.

Children

This tool is intended for adults planning events. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Contact

If the site owner has configured a feedback link, it appears in the site footer.
